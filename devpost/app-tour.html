Runs on its own

Emails that send themselves

JudgesAn invite when the phase starts, a note each morning a batch opens, and a last call on the due date.
OwnerA heads-up when a phase is due, and when it's judged, with a reminder every 2 days until it's closed.
TeamsA winner or thank-you email, when you press send.

They run as durable workflows, and each send is keyed, so a retry never sends it twice.
