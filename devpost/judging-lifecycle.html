01

The life of a hackathon

Round one goes to the agent. The ideas go to people.
1
Teams

Projects come in

  • From Devpost through the Intake API
  • Or the submission form
  • Shaped by the schema: repo, video, demo URL, team
2
Organizer

Set up judging

  • Criteria, weights and gates
  • How the agent checks each one
  • Phases, judges and daily batches
3
Agent

Round one

  • Reviews every project, one criterion at a time
  • Builds the repo, reads the code, visits the demo, reads the transcript
  • Checks every gate
4
Judges

Judges score

  • An email when each batch opens
  • Submission and rubric on one page
  • Agent's score shown only after they submit
5
Organizer

Close the phase

  • Rank: gates, then the judges' average
  • The agent's score only breaks ties
  • Top projects advance
6
Teams and public

Results

  • Prize tiers and awards
  • Winner and thank-you emails
  • Public winners page

Failed a gate? Inbox.

A person sees the agent's evidence, then agrees or puts the project back in the pool.

↻  Next phase. The pool shrinks each round until the final panel picks the winners.
Chat
Ask across every project. Nemotron with RAG over each submission and the pages it links to. Scope by phase, judge, status or a hand-picked set.
Audit trail
Every decision, on the record. Submissions, agent verdicts, overrides, judge scores, inbox calls, moves between rounds and awards.
