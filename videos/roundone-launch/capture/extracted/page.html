The new normal

Building got cheap. Judging didn't.

When a weekend team can ship a working app with a coding agent, “does it run?” stops separating the field. What separates projects now is what a person brought: the problem they picked, the insight, the taste.

Ten times the pile, same three judges

Submissions multiplied. The panel didn’t. At a careful eight minutes a look, 1,000 projects is 400 judge-hours, and in practice nobody runs the code. The projects at the bottom of the pile get the tired judges.

The split

Let the machine check the machine.

Save people for what people made. The agent takes every criterion a machine can verify, and the ones only people can score stay with your judges.

The agent checks

  • Does it build and pass its tests?
  • Does it use the sponsor tech?
  • Does the demo match the claims?
  • Did it follow the rules?

Humans judge

  • Is the idea new?
  • Would it matter to anyone?
  • Is it made with taste?
  • Would you use it?

Humans in the loop

The agent does the homework. People make the call.

Judges' average scores set the ranking. The agent's score only breaks ties, and it never picks a winner.

  1. 01

    Setup

    YouWrite the rubric and the agent's guidance in your own words. Keep any criterion human only.

    AgentLearns what to look for, criterion by criterion.

  2. 02

    Screening

    YouRule on the projects the agent fails on a gate.

    AgentChecks every project against your gates and shows its evidence.

  3. 03

    Judging

    YouScore first, then compare. Gaps of 2 points or more get flagged.

    AgentScores what it can check, with its reasoning.

  4. 04

    Advancing

    YouDecide how many projects move on, and close each round.

    AgentBreaks ties in the ranking. Nothing more.

  5. 05

    Results

    YouPick the award winners, email them and publish the winners page.

    AgentStays out of it.

Open stack

Open models, on infrastructure you own

The judging agent runs on open models and open infrastructure, from the first build check to the last prior-art search.

Every verdict

Nemotron

Pick how much model each criterion gets. Every tier is Nemotron.

  • QuickNemotron 3.5 LightningOne fast pass over what the mechanisms found
  • BalancedNemotron 3 Super 120BChecks a few things itself with tools. Also runs the chat
  • In-depthNemotron 3 Ultra 550BThe largest model, with room to dig

Double check: when the agent is unsure, a larger Nemotron takes a second look.

Inference and sandboxes

Token Factory

Token Factory serves every model call and the embeddings behind chat search. Each repo builds in a Nebius Sandbox: VM-isolated, with every review step on its own fork of the image.

The live web

Tavily

  • Extract reads a project's pages
  • Crawl walks the live demo, following your instructions
  • Search looks for prior art: does this idea already exist?

Tavily is how RoundOne checks originality, which is the whole point.

Own itOpen source and self-hosted. Your brand, your database, and any model swapped with one environment variable.

Pick how each criterion gets checked

Self-hosted

Run it on your own stack

RoundOne is a Next.js app on Supabase. Deploy it for your org, choose the models the agent runs on and put your name, color and logo on every page from one config file.

  • Your brand on the app, the judge portal and the winners page
  • Submissions and scores stay in your own database
  • Documented end to end, down to every page and API
Deployment guide
// branding.config.js
const branding = {
  name: "Acme Hacks",

};
