General holds the hackathon's basics: its name, tagline and dates, which stage it's in, whether the winners page is public, and the option to delete it. The page header shows the hackathon's admin address, `/h/<slug>`.

## Details

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Required, up to 120 characters. It's the same name you can edit on [Branding](/docs/setup/branding). |
| **Tagline** | Optional, up to 200 characters. It shows on your hackathon list and the submission form. The agent also reads the name and tagline for context when it judges. |
| **Dates** | The start and end dates. The end can't be before the start. They show on your hackathon list and the winners page. |
| **Stage** | **Setup**, **Judging** or **Results**. See below. |
| **Winners page is public** | Makes the public winners page, `/w/<slug>`, visible to anyone. See below. |

Click **Save changes** to save. **Saved** appears next to the button when it's done.

## The hackathon's URL

The slug in `/h/<slug>`, `/w/<slug>` and the other addresses comes from the name you gave the hackathon when you created it. If another hackathon already had that slug, a short random suffix was added. Renaming the hackathon later doesn't change its slug, so links you've shared keep working.

## Stage

The app moves the stage for you as judging goes along:

| When you… | Stage becomes |
| --- | --- |
| Start judging on [Progress](/docs/judging/progress) | **Judging** |
| Close the last phase | **Results** |
| Reset judging | **Setup** |

The stage decides which area opens when you go to `/h/<slug>`, the badge on your hackathon list, and whether you can publish results from [Winners](/docs/results/winners), which needs **Results**. You can also set it by hand here.

## Winners page

**Winners page is public** is the same switch as **Publish results** on [Winners](/docs/results/winners). There's one difference: **Publish results** only works once the last phase has closed, and this checkbox doesn't check.

Publishing makes the page public. It doesn't send any emails: you send those separately from [Winner emails](/docs/results/emails). Resetting judging takes the page down again.

## Delete the hackathon

Under **Danger zone**, click **Delete** and confirm **Delete hackathon**. This removes the hackathon for everyone, including its public winners page, and takes you back to your hackathon list.

<Callout type="warning">

Deleting a hackathon can't be undone.

</Callout>
