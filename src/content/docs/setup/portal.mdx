The judge portal is what a judge sees when they open their private link. This page sets the words on its start and finish screens, shows a live preview, and lists every judge's link in one place.

The page has two tabs: **Portal settings** and **Judge links**.

## Portal settings

Everything here is optional. Leave it blank and judges still get a working portal with a greeting, their queue and the criteria.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Headline** | The big heading on the start screen. `{name}` becomes the judge's first name, as in `Welcome, {name}`. Leave it empty to greet each judge with "Welcome" and their first name. Up to 120 characters. |
| **Welcome message** | A note from the organizers under the headline. Up to 2,000 characters. |
| **Remember the goal** | Up to 8 short points judges read before they start, 200 characters each. Click **Add a goal** to add one. You don't need to repeat the criteria: judges see those anyway. |
| **When they finish** | A message on the finish screen, such as when winners are announced. Up to 1,000 characters. |

Click **Update** in the page header to save. Empty goals are dropped when you save. A dot on the **Portal settings** tab means you have unsaved changes.

The **Welcome message** and goals also appear in the email judges get with their link when a phase starts, if email is set up on your server.

## Preview

The preview beside the form updates as you type. Switch between **Start** and **Finish** to see each screen. It uses your first judge's first name, a few of your real project names, the running phase (or the first one before judging starts) and your cadence from [Distribution](/docs/setup/distribution).

## What judges see

The start screen shows, from top to bottom:

- The phase and round, such as "Group review · round 1 of 2".
- Your **Headline**.
- How many projects they have to judge. With daily batches, it counts today's and says how many more open over the coming days.
- Your **Welcome message** and **Remember the goal** points.
- A **Start judging** button (**Keep going** once they've begun), with a time estimate of about 4 minutes per project.
- **What you'll score**: each criterion with its weight and rubric. Pass/fail criteria are marked **pass / fail**, and gates **must pass**.
- Their projects, with a check and score next to each one they've done.

The finish screen appears once they've scored everything open to them. It thanks them, shows your **When they finish** message and lists their scores. With batches still to come, it offers the next batch early. Otherwise it tells them they can still change a score until you close the round.

For how judges score from there, see [the judge's page](/docs/links/judge-portal).

## Judge links

The **Judge links** tab lists every judge with their link, `/j/<token>`, and buttons to copy it, open it, or make a **New link**. The count on the tab is how many judges have a link.

- A link opens only that judge's queue, and anyone who has it can score as them. Send each one directly.
- If a link leaks, click **New link** and confirm. The old link stops working straight away.
- The same link works in every phase. It shows whatever that judge has in the phase that's running.

**Copy all links** copies one line per judge with their name, email and full link, separated by tabs, ready to paste into a spreadsheet.

To add judges, go to [Judges](/docs/setup/judges). Each one gets a link when you save them.
