**Your hackathons** is the home screen. It lists every hackathon you created, newest first, with its stage and how far judging has got. From here you open a hackathon or start a new one.

The list only holds your own hackathons. Each hackathon belongs to the organizer who created it, so other organizers on the same instance have their own lists.

If you open `/` while signed out, you're sent to the sign-in screen. See [Sign in and settings](/docs/workspace/account). On the official RoundOne site, signed-out visitors see the landing page instead; see [Configuration](/docs/reference/configuration#landing-page-and-site-hosts).

## The summary line

Under the heading, a line counts your hackathons ("events") and the total number of projects across all of them.

## Cards and list views

The **Cards** and **List** switch at the top right changes how the hackathons are shown. The page opens in Cards view each time you load it.

### Cards

Each card shows:

- The hackathon's banner, or a generated pixel cover in its color when there's no banner.
- Its logo, or initials when there's no logo, and its tagline.
- Its name, dates ("Dates TBD" when none are set), and its project and judge counts.
- A status badge. While judging runs, the card also shows the percent reviewed and a progress bar. Once results are published, the badge has a check mark and the card shows a **Winners page** line.

The last tile, **Start a hackathon**, opens [Create a hackathon](/docs/workspace/new-hackathon).

### List

The list is a table with **Name**, **Dates**, **Stage**, **Progress**, **Projects** and **Judges** columns. With no hackathons yet, it shows "No hackathons yet." and a **Start one** link.

## Status

The status badge combines the hackathon's stage with where judging stands.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Setup — draft | Judging hasn't started. |
| Judging — *phase name* | Judging is running, in the named phase. |
| Judging | The stage is Judging but no phase is running. |
| Results — draft | The last phase has closed and results aren't published yet. |
| Results published | The public winners page is live. |

See [Key concepts](/docs/concepts#stage) for how a hackathon moves between stages.

## Progress

Progress is the share of the running phase's judge assignments that have been submitted. Reviews an organizer adds as their own don't count. It reads 0% before judging starts and 100% once the hackathon reaches Results.

## Opening a hackathon

Click a card or a name in the list. The hackathon opens in the area that matches its stage:

| Stage | Opens on |
| --- | --- |
| Setup | Setup › Schema |
| Judging | Judging › Projects |
| Results | Results › Winners |

To come back here from inside a hackathon, click the logo at the top left.

## New hackathon

Click **New hackathon** at the top right, or the **Start a hackathon** tile, to create one. See [Create a hackathon](/docs/workspace/new-hackathon).
